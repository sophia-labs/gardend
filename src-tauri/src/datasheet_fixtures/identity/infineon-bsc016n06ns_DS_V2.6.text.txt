## MOSFET

OptiMOS™ Power-MOSFET, 60 V

## Features

- Optimized for synchronous rectification ·
- 175°C rated ·
- 100% avalanche tested ·
- Superior thermal resistance ·
- N-channel ·
- Pb-free lead plating; RoHS compliant ·
